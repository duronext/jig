01Rules

Four rules every PRD page follows

RULE 01

One sentence first

The thesis plate is written before anything else. No sentence, no page.

RULE 02

Colour means something

Indigo is the work, pink is a change or a decision, dashed grey is outside. Nothing is coloured to look nice.

RULE 03

Say it where it is

Mock, planned and unchecked are marked in the row itself, not in a note three sections away.

RULE 04

Anyone can read it

If a step needs logic, write plain steps. A reader who has never coded should follow every line.

principlesThree or four cells. Only for decisions that would change the page if you broke them.

06Today

Today a failed card costs a person two hours

Most failed cards go through on a second try. Today nobody tries again until someone in finance notices.

Today

  1. The card fails overnight. Nothing happens.
  2. The customer gets an automatic "payment failed" email every day.
  3. Finance finds the invoice in a weekly export.
  4. Someone emails the customer, waits, and retries by hand.
~2 h a day of finance time · 5 emails per customer
→

With this

  1. The card fails overnight. It is tried again the next day.
  2. Most invoices are paid on the second or third try. Nobody is told.
  3. Only if all three tries fail does the customer get one email.
  4. The email's link lets them fix the card from their phone.
0 finance time for the first three tries · 1 email
before / afterSame number of steps on each side so the reader can compare line by line. The cost line carries units.

13Scope

What is in, what waits, and what we won't build

In the MVP

  • Three retries on a fixed schedule
  • One email after the last try
  • A fix-it page without sign-in
  • A new "Retrying" status in the invoice list

Waits

  • Weekly finance summary · Next
  • Text reminders · needs phone numbers
  • Custom retry days · only if asked

Not building

  • Retrying a different card on file · customers never agreed to it
  • Pausing the account on failure · a separate decision for finance
scopeThree columns, always in this order. Every "waits" and "not building" item says why in grey.

17Callouts

Four callouts, one meaning each

Note · indigo

A retry never charges twice

Each try checks the invoice is still unpaid before it charges. If the customer paid some other way, the try is skipped.

Changed in rev C · pink

The finance summary moved to Next

Cutting it ships the MVP a week sooner. Finance agreed on 2026-10-08 as long as the invoice list shows the new status.

Outside · phantom

The card processor decides what "declined" means

We don't control their reasons or their timing. Some declines, like a stolen card, should never be retried, and the processor tells us which.

Risk · red

A retry on an account that just closed

If a customer cancels between tries, the next try could charge a closed account. Each try checks the account is still active.

calloutNote, changed, outside, risk. Do not invent a fifth. The eyebrow names the kind so it reads without colour too.

24Close

The title block closes every PRD

Engineering drawings end with who drew it, who checked it and who approved it. So does every Jig PRD. The stamp says what the page is good for right now.

Dunning
Chasing an unpaid invoice. Today it is a person; with this it is a schedule.
Retry
Charging the same card again, a few days later, without asking the customer.
Fix-it link
A link in the email that opens one page for changing the card, no password needed.
Recovered
An invoice that was declined at first and paid later with nobody stepping in.
Will a customer ever be charged twice?

No. Each try checks the invoice is still unpaid first. See the note in the callouts.

Can a customer turn retries off?

Not in the MVP. It is on the Later list and only gets built if beta customers ask for it.

What about invoices already failing today?

They stay with finance. Retries only start for charges that fail after launch.
