Self-healing invoices

A failed card retries before anyone notices.

Finance spends about two hours a day chasing cards that would have gone through on a second try. This PRD makes the second and third try automatic, sends the customer one clear email when that is not enough, and leaves finance only the invoices a person has to handle.

The one sentence
When a card fails, the invoice fixes itself before anyone has to chase it.

02Today

Today a failed card costs a person two hours

Most failed cards go through on a second try. Today nobody tries again until someone in finance notices.

Today

  1. The card fails overnight. Nothing happens.
  2. The customer gets an automatic "payment failed" email every day.
  3. Finance finds the invoice in a weekly export.
  4. Someone emails the customer, waits, and retries by hand.
~2 h a day of finance time · 5 emails per customer
→

With this

  1. The card fails overnight. It is tried again the next day.
  2. Most invoices are paid on the second or third try. Nobody is told.
  3. Only if all three tries fail does the customer get one email.
  4. The email's link lets them fix the card from their phone.
0 finance time for the first three tries · 1 email

  try again right away

07Scope

What is in, what waits, and what we won't build

In the MVP

  • Three retries on a fixed schedule
  • One email after the last try
  • A fix-it page without sign-in
  • A new "Retrying" status in the invoice list

Waits

  • Weekly finance summary · Next
  • Text reminders · needs phone numbers
  • Custom retry days · only if asked

Not building

  • Retrying a different card on file · customers never agreed to it
  • Pausing the account on failure · a separate decision for finance
